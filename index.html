When to Call Me

Here's when founders typically reach out

01

Founder Burnout / When You Can't Think Clearly Anymore

"I'm still running the company. But I don't fully trust myself anymore."

Burnout, anxiety, decision paralysis, mild depression, and loneliness at the top don't always look like collapse. Often, they show up while the founder is still fundraising, hiring, selling, leading, and carrying everyone else.

I work with founders who are still performing on the outside — but feel foggy, reactive, isolated, or unable to trust their own judgment — so they can think clearly again, make better calls, and drive business results.

02

Co-Founder 'Couples' Therapy / When You Can't Communicate

"We built this company together. Now every real conversation either escalates — or doesn't happen at all."

Different communication styles, unspoken resentment, old baggage, broken trust, unclear ownership, and power struggles eventually become business problems.

I work with founders to turn unresolved tension into operational decisions: who owns what, who decides what, how conflict is handled, and what each founder needs to change.

03

Scaling the Company / When the Company Grows Faster Than the People, Structure, and Processes

"The company is growing. But everything around it is still built for the previous stage."

Growth is the goal — now the founders, leadership team, org structure, processes, communication rhythms, and decision-making habits all need to scale with the business.

I help founders build the leadership and organizational capacity required for the next stage — without killing speed, ownership, or the culture that got them here.

04

Business Stagnation / When the Business Should Be Moving — But Isn't

"The market is there. The product is strong. The company is funded. So why aren't we growing?"

Some companies have real potential: a big market, a differentiated product, strong investors, and enough capital to move. But growth still stalls. Decisions slow down, momentum gets lost, and the same strategic questions keep coming back without resolution.

I work with founders to identify where stagnation is being created at the top: how they communicate, make decisions, resolve tension, and operate — or fail to operate — as one leadership body.
